# SaaS Launch Kit: research (17 Oct 2026)

Desk research for a packaged launch offer (brand, launch site, launch film, AI search setup, then an
optional monthly plan). Every figure below came from a linked source in the research run; prices
are vendors' own published figures. Not yet a decision.

## What comparable offers charge
- Designjoy: unlimited design requests, $4,995/mo, pause any time, "75% back" in week one
  (designjoy.co). Reported $3.1M revenue in 2024 as a one-person studio (Latka, third-party).
- Awesomic: design subscription incl. launch sites and video, $1,490 to $2,995/mo.
- Lotiva: brand plus landing page $3,500; guidelines plus 5-page site plus launch kit $7,500.
- Marque (NZ): identity plus site from $7,500 to $20,000. Ayko (UK): 2-week Webflow site GBP 5K to 10K.
- Hound Studio: 60 s launch film $3,900 (view-count guarantees on higher tiers). Flowjam: from $15,000.
- LoudFace: SEO plus AEO retainer $5K to $18K+/mo, 3-month minimum; audit $2K to $5K.
- Indian studios: explainer films about $1,000 to $2,500; site build figures unreliable.

## Signals
- No visible offer bundles launch site + AEO + launch film; Webflow AEO agencies skip video, video
  studios skip AEO. (Not finding one is not proof none exists.)
- Founders' agency complaints (HN): slow feedback, no docs at handoff, code they can't change,
  costly post-launch changes. YC advises against outsourcing the product build, not marketing.
- A money-back line next to the CTA lifted sales 29% in one test (Conversion Rate Experts).
- Equity-for-services: venture studios take 30 to 80%; advisers recommend cash plus small vested
  equity, never equity alone.

## Proposal (inference)
- Kit: brand basics, 5-page launch site, 60 s film plus cuts, AEO setup with a before/after score;
  app builds and 3D as add-ons. One fixed fee, roughly $8K to $15K (the pieces cost $13K to $16K
  separately from US vendors).
- Optional monthly plan about $2K to $4K/mo, 3-month minimum.
- Guarantee: fixed launch date with money back per week late, next to the CTA; full source, docs
  and repo ownership at handoff.
- Headline direction: "Launch-ready in 4 weeks: brand, site, launch film, and found in ChatGPT on
  day one."

## Rigan to decide
Timeline and kits per month; whether to refund for delays; price point; equity (yes/no, cash
floor); proof of AEO results to show; build tool (code, Framer or Webflow).
