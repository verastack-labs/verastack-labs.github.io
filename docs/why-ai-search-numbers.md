# Why AI search matters: sourced numbers

Evidence sheet for the free AI-search tools on verastack.dev. Compiled 18 Oct 2026.

Rules used: every figure below was read on the publisher's own page, or on a major outlet or wire
syndication quoting the publisher directly (noted where that applies). Company-reported user counts
are self-reported, not audited. Anything that could not be traced is in "Do not use" at the end.

## 1. How many people use AI assistants and AI search

- **900 million weekly active users of ChatGPT**, plus 50 million paying subscribers. OpenAI
  announcement, reported by TechCrunch, which links OpenAI's post "Scaling AI for everyone" (the
  OpenAI page itself blocks automated reading). (TechCrunch quoting OpenAI, 27 Feb 2026)
  https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/27/chatgpt-reaches-900m-weekly-active-users
- **AI Overviews: over 2.5 billion monthly active users.** Sundar Pichai, I/O 2026 keynote.
  (Google, 19 May 2026) https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/sundar-pichai-io-2026/
- **AI Mode: surpassed 1 billion monthly active users** about a year after launch. Same keynote,
  repeated in the Q2 2026 earnings remarks ("Since expanding AI Mode globally last October, we've
  surpassed 1 billion monthly active users"). (Google, 19 May 2026 and 22 Jul 2026)
  https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/sundar-pichai-io-2026/
- **Gemini app: 950 million monthly active users**, with daily active users tripling in a year
  (up from 400 million at I/O 2025 and "over 900 million" at I/O 2026). Q2 2026 earnings remarks.
  (Google / Alphabet, 22 Jul 2026)
  https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/message-ceo/alphabet-earnings-q2-2026/
- **Perplexity: 780 million queries in May 2025**, growing more than 20% month over month. CEO
  Aravind Srinivas on stage at Bloomberg Tech Summit. Perplexity has published no newer official
  figure that we could find. (TechCrunch quoting Perplexity's CEO, 5 Jun 2025)
  https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/05/perplexity-received-780-million-queries-last-month-ceo-says/

Note: ChatGPT reports weekly users, Google reports monthly users. Do not compare them as if
they were the same measure.

## 2. AI assistants in product research and buying decisions

Consumer:

- **58% of consumers have replaced traditional search engines with generative AI tools** as their
  go-to for product and service recommendations (up from 25% in 2023). Survey of 12,000 consumers
  aged 18+ in 12 countries, Oct to Nov 2024. (Capgemini Research Institute, "What Matters to
  Today's Consumer", 9 Jan 2025)
  https://www.capgemini.com/news/press-releases/71-of-consumers-want-generative-ai-integrated-into-their-shopping-experiences/
- **39% of US consumers had used generative AI for online shopping**, 53% planned to that year.
  Among users: 55% for research, 47% for product recommendations, 43% for deals. Survey of 5,000
  US consumers. (Adobe, 17 Mar 2025)
  https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2025/03/17/adobe-analytics-traffic-to-us-retail-websites-from-generative-ai-sources-jumps-1200-percent
- **About 80% of search users rely on AI summaries or "zero-click" results for at least 40% of
  their searches**; Bain estimates this cuts organic web traffic by 15% to 25%. **42% of LLM users
  ask for shopping recommendations.** Bain-Dynata Generative AI Consumer Survey, n=1,117, Dec 2024
  (country not stated). Self-reported behaviour; the traffic cut is Bain's estimate.
  (Bain & Company, 19 Feb 2025)
  https://www.bain.com/about/media-center/press-releases/20252/consumer-reliance-on-ai-search-results-signals-new-era-of-marketing--bain--company-about-80-of-search-users-rely-on-ai-summaries-at-least-40-of-the-time-on-traditional-search-engines-about-60-of-searches-now-end-without-the-user-progressing-to-a/
  and https://www.bain.com/insights/goodbye-clicks-hello-ai-zero-click-search-redefines-marketing/

B2B: see section 5.

## 3. Organic click-through when a Google AI Overview appears

- **Users clicked a traditional result link on 8% of visits when an AI summary appeared, versus
  15% when none appeared.** Clicks on links inside the summary: 1% of visits. Sessions ended on
  26% of pages with a summary versus 16% without. 18% of the 68,879 searches studied produced a
  summary. Browsing data from 900 US adults (KnowledgePanel Digital), March 2025. Observational.
  (Pew Research Center, 22 Jul 2025)
  https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/
- **An AI Overview correlates with a 58% lower average CTR for the top-ranking page.** 300,000
  keywords (150,000 with an AI Overview, 150,000 informational without), aggregated Google Search
  Console data, Dec 2023 versus Dec 2025, desktop position-one CTR. Correlational. Replaces the
  April 2025 version of the same study, which found 34.5%. (Ahrefs, 4 Feb 2026)
  https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks-update/
- **Organic CTR on queries with an AI Overview fell from 1.76% (Jun 2024) to 0.61% (Sep 2025),
  a stated 61% drop; paid CTR on the same queries fell 68% (19.7% to 6.34%).** Queries without an
  AI Overview also fell (organic 2.72% to 1.62%), so not all of the drop is the Overview. Brands
  cited inside the Overview saw higher CTR than uncited ones (Q3 2025 organic 0.70% vs 0.52%), but
  Seer says it cannot prove citation causes that. 3,119 informational queries across 42 client
  organisations, 25.1M organic impressions. The article contains some internal inconsistencies, so
  quote the headline figures only. (Seer Interactive, 4 Nov 2025)
  https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-september-2025-update

## 4. How AI-referred visitors behave and convert

- **AI referrals converted 31% more than non-AI sources over the 2025 holiday season**, drove 32%
  more revenue per visit, 14% higher engagement and 45% longer time on site. AI-driven retail
  traffic was up 693% year over year (Nov to Dec 2025). Adobe Analytics, US retail sites,
  1 Nov to 31 Dec 2025. (Marketing Dive quoting Adobe, 23 Jan 2026)
  https://www.marketingdive.com/news/ai-has-changed-holiday-shopping-heres-what-the-numbers-say/810265/
- **Context for the above: in early 2025 the same Adobe data showed AI traffic 9% less likely to
  convert** than other traffic (43% less in July 2024), with 23% lower bounce rate and 8% higher
  engagement. The gap closed and reversed during 2025. (Adobe, 17 Mar 2025)
  https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2025/03/17/adobe-analytics-traffic-to-us-retail-websites-from-generative-ai-sources-jumps-1200-percent
- **On ahrefs.com, AI search sent 0.5% of visits but 12.1% of signups**, a conversion rate about
  23x that of traditional organic search. One site, last 30 days. Single-company data, not a
  market study. (Ahrefs, 16 Jun 2025)
  https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-search-traffic-conversions-ahrefs/
- **The average AI search visitor is "4.4 times as valuable" as an organic visitor, "based on
  conversion rate."** No sample, date range or underlying rates disclosed. Weak; use only with the
  caveat. (Semrush, 21 Jul 2025) https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-search-seo-traffic-study/

## 5. B2B buyers using generative AI to research vendors

- **94% of B2B buyers use AI in their buying process**, up five points from 89% a year earlier;
  twice as many buyers as before named generative AI or conversational search as their most
  important information source, ahead of vendor websites and sales. Forrester Buyers' Journey
  Survey, 2025. (Forrester, John Buten, 22 Jan 2026)
  https://www.forrester.com/blogs/b2b_buyers_make_zero_click_buying_number_one/
- **51% of B2B software buyers now start research with an AI chatbot more often than with
  Google** (29% in 2025). 71% use AI chatbots at some point; 85% think more highly of a vendor when
  AI includes it in an answer; 69% chose a different vendor than first planned because of a
  chatbot recommendation. Survey of 1,076 B2B software buyers, March 2026. Note G2 sells review
  and AI-visibility products. (G2, "The Answer Economy", 15 Apr 2026)
  https://company.g2.com/news/g2-research-the-answer-economy
- **45% of B2B buyers used GenAI in a recent purchase, primarily to gather information on vendors
  and products**; 69% prefer to validate AI-generated insights with sales reps. Survey of 645 B2B
  buyers, Aug to Sep 2025. Gartner's newsroom blocks automated reading; text read from the Business
  Wire release as syndicated by 01net. (Gartner, 20 May 2026)
  https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-20-gartner-survey-finds-sixty-nine-percent-of-b-two-b-buyers-turn-to-sales-reps-to-validate-ai-generated-insights
  (read via https://www.01net.it/gartner-survey-finds-69-of-b2b-buyers-turn-to-sales-reps-to-validate-ai-generated-insights/)
- **72% of tech buyers encountered Google AI Overviews during research; 90% of those clicked
  through to cited sources.** Survey of over 2,500 technology buyers and vendors. TrustRadius page
  blocked; read via trade outlet. ("Clicked through" is not defined as always or sometimes.)
  (Digital Commerce 360 quoting TrustRadius, 16 Apr 2025)
  https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2025/04/16/ai-disrupts-b2b-buying-trust-drives-tech-purchase-decisions/amp/

## 6. Structured data, FAQs and being cited in AI answers

Strict reading: there is no credible evidence that adding schema markup or FAQ schema, by itself,
raises the chance of being cited. The best controlled test found no uplift. Pitch structured data
as hygiene and clarity, not as a citation lever.

- **Google: "There are no additional technical requirements" for AI Overviews and AI Mode, and
  "no special schema.org structured data that you need to add."** Google does recommend that
  structured data match the visible text. Official guidance. (Google Search Central, last updated
  10 Dec 2025) https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features
- **Controlled test: adding JSON-LD produced no meaningful citation uplift.** 1,885 pages that added
  JSON-LD (Aug 2025 to Mar 2026) versus matched controls, 30 days before and after: AI Mode +2.4%
  and ChatGPT +2.2% (both indistinguishable from zero), AI Overviews -4.6% (significant, cause
  unclear). Only pages already heavily cited were studied; no FAQ-specific result. Quasi-experimental
  (difference-in-differences). (Ahrefs, 11 May 2026) https://ahrefs.com/blog/schema-ai-citations/
- **Correlational: AI-cited pages were almost three times more likely to have JSON-LD** than
  non-cited pages (initial analysis of 6 million URLs). Ahrefs itself attributes this to overall
  site quality, not schema. Correlation only. (Ahrefs, 11 May 2026)
  https://ahrefs.com/blog/schema-ai-citations/
- **Correlational: "schema markup has no measurable effect on LLM visibility"** across OpenAI,
  Gemini and Perplexity, comparing domains bucketed by schema coverage. Sample size not stated;
  vendor study. (Search Atlas, 14 Dec 2025)
  https://searchatlas.com/research/the-limits-of-schema-markup-for-ai-search/
- **Experimental, content not markup: adding citations, quotations and statistics to content
  boosted visibility in generative engine responses by up to 40%** on a research benchmark
  (GEO-bench). Peer-reviewed (KDD 2024), lab setting, not live Google or ChatGPT. (Aggarwal et al.,
  Princeton and others, arXiv 2311.09735, rev. 28 Jun 2024) https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735v3

## 7. Gartner's search volume prediction

- **"By 2026, traditional search engine volume will drop 25%, with search marketing losing market
  share to AI chatbots and other virtual agents, according to Gartner, Inc."** Alan Antin, VP
  Analyst: "Generative AI (GenAI) solutions are becoming substitute answer engines, replacing user
  queries that previously may have been executed in traditional search engines." Gartner's page
  blocks automated reading; wording read from Slashdot's verbatim reproduction of the release.
  (Gartner press release, 19 Feb 2024)
  https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2024-02-19-gartner-predicts-search-engine-volume-will-drop-25-percent-by-2026-due-to-ai-chatbots-and-other-virtual-agents
  (read via https://search.slashdot.org/story/24/03/04/170221/gartner-predicts-search-engine-volume-will-drop-25-by-2026-due-to-ai-chatbots-and-other-virtual-agents)
- Caution: it is a 2024 forecast, not a measurement, and 2026 is now. Google reports Search queries
  growing, not shrinking (Q2 2026 remarks). Cite it as "Gartner predicted in 2024", never as "search
  volume has dropped 25%". Some outlets misstate it as a 25% drop in traffic to websites; that is
  not the wording.

## Best five for tool pages

1. **Google: AI Overviews over 2.5 billion monthly users; AI Mode over 1 billion** (Google I/O,
   May 2026). First-party, huge, current.
2. **Pew: clicks on results drop from 15% to 8% of visits when an AI summary appears; 1% click a
   cited link** (Jul 2025). Independent, measured behaviour, not a vendor.
3. **ChatGPT: 900 million weekly users** (OpenAI via TechCrunch, Feb 2026).
4. **Forrester: 94% of B2B buyers use AI in buying; twice as many now call it their most important
   source** (Jan 2026). Pair with Gartner's 45% (measured on one recent purchase) if a more
   conservative number is wanted.
5. **Adobe: AI-referred shoppers converted 31% more than non-AI traffic, holiday 2025** (Jan 2026).
   Largest dataset of any conversion figure here.

## Do not use

- **"Search traffic will drop 25% by 2026" (as fact or as a traffic figure).** Gartner predicted
  search engine volume, not website traffic, and it was a forecast. See section 7.
- **ChatGPT "1 billion monthly active users."** Aggregator estimate; OpenAI reports weekly users
  and we found no OpenAI statement of 1B monthly.
- **Gemini app "1 billion monthly users" (Aug 2026).** Only on aggregator and news-digest sites;
  could not trace to a Google post. Latest verified: 950M (Jul 2026).
- **Perplexity "1B+ monthly queries", "100M monthly users", or 34M/45M MAU.** Third-party
  estimates that contradict each other; no company disclosure found.
- **Similarweb "ChatGPT referrals convert at 11.4% vs 5.3% organic" (also quoted as 7.1%).** Only
  on secondary blogs with conflicting numbers; could not find Similarweb's original.
- **"FAQ schema makes pages 3.2x (or 2.7x, or 41% vs 15%) more likely to be cited."** No primary
  dataset found; contradicted by the Ahrefs controlled test.
- **Semrush "structured data gives +22% citation lift" (304,805 URLs).** Could not find the study.
- **"Schema gives 300% higher accuracy in LLMs."** Untraceable.
- **Microsoft/Bing "schema helps our LLMs understand content" (Fabrice Canel, SMX Munich, Mar
  2025).** Real quote per secondary reports, but the Search Engine Land report could not be read
  here, it is qualitative, and Canel has since retired. Not a citation-rate figure.
- **McKinsey "half of consumers use AI-powered search" / "$750bn through AI search by 2028" /
  "20 to 50% traffic loss" (Oct 2025).** Likely real, but McKinsey's page would not load, so the
  wording, sample and base are unverified. Read it before using.
- **TrustRadius 2026 "63% of buyers use AI" (Jul 2026).** Only seen in a secondary summary.
- **Search Engine Land "ChatGPT converts worse than organic" (973 ecommerce sites).** Page blocked;
  not read.
- **Adobe "AI revenue per visit up 254%."** Seen only in search snippets of Adobe's blog, which
  timed out; use the Marketing Dive figures instead.

## 8. AI crawlers and JavaScript (added 18 Oct 2026)

- **"None of the major AI crawlers currently render JavaScript"**, naming OpenAI's crawlers,
  ClaudeBot, Meta-ExternalAgent, Bytespider and PerplexityBot; ChatGPT's and Claude's crawlers fetch
  JavaScript files but do not execute them. Gemini (via Googlebot) and AppleBot do render. Vercel
  network data, past month. (Vercel, 17 Dec 2024; dated, re-check before quoting as current)
  https://vercel.com/blog/the-rise-of-the-ai-crawler
