# SaaS Launch Kit (decided 18 Oct 2026)

Rigan's decisions on the packaged launch offer. The research behind them is in
`docs/launch-kit-research.md`.

## What it is
Brand basics, a 5-page launch site, a 60-second launch film with cuts for social, and AEO and GEO
setup, in 4 weeks for one fixed fee. App builds and 3D are add-ons, quoted separately.

## Decisions
| | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Price | $9,500 fixed. 50% to start, 50% at launch. |
| Timeline and capacity | 4 weeks. Two kits a month (one starts every two weeks); the page can say "two launch slots a month". |
| Guarantee | 10% of the fee back for each week we are late, capped at 30%. The clock pauses while we wait on the client's feedback. Full source, docs and repo ownership at handoff. Shown next to the buy button. |
| Equity | Not advertised. Case by case for a startup we believe in: at least 60% of the fee in cash, the rest as up to 1% equity via a SAFE, vesting over 2 years. Never equity alone. |
| AEO and GEO proof | We promise the launch site scores 90 or more on our check, with a before and after. Proof: our own sites at 100 and the studies. No promise of being named in AI answers. Headline wording: "built to be found by ChatGPT from day one", not "found on day one". |
| Build tool | Code (Next.js) by default; the client owns the repo. Framer on request, when their team wants to edit pages without a developer. |
| Monthly plan | $2,500/mo, 3-month minimum, then pause any time. Site changes and new pages, monthly AEO and GEO tracking (the engines asked the buyers' questions again), and two short cut-downs or new clips a month. |
