A. Hand-over

The section pins, and as you scroll the studio hands the whole screen to each product in turn. The background, text and accent shift into rigseed's slate, then Riggit's green, then flip to Mehfil's cream and neobrutal borders, with a live mockup of each app. It shows that the studio builds distinct brands, not one template, and the colour shift is a scroll moment in itself.

built from: GSAP ScrollTrigger pin + colour tweening on CSS variables · custom product mockups
