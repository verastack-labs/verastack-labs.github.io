A. Cinema reel

Each headline project starts as a framed card and grows to fill the screen as you scroll, with its name in huge type and the credit line ("as an employee", "freelance") in mono. Then the supporting projects arrive as a list: hover a row and a chartreuse band slides up with a scrolling marquee. It's the most cinematic, and it gives the two live launches the weight they deserve.

built from: GSAP ScrollTrigger pin + scale (like React Bits Scroll Expand) · React Bits Flowing Menu for the list

B. Horizontal reel

The section pins and vertical scrolling moves a strip of projects sideways, big cards for the launches and smaller ones for the rest. It feels like a showreel and fits everything in one pass. It's a popular pattern, so it's less of a moment than A, and the small projects get similar billing to the big ones.

built from: GSAP ScrollTrigger horizontal pin (the pattern in 21st.dev hyperiux timeline)

C. Founder's log

A git-log style list, one row per project, newest first. Click a row and it opens to show a recording, a line of context and the links. It's the most honest to the "our founder" voice and very on-brand for a code-first studio, and it scales as the list grows. It's the least cinematic of the three.

built from: custom accordion (Motion layout animation) + React Bits Decrypted Text on open
